Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers.

Here are a few things customers often want to know before booking a service.

What types of computer problems do you fix?

We handle laptop and desktop issues including power failures, damaged displays, overheating, slow performance, software errors, and board-level hardware faults.

Do you provide both laptop and desktop repair services?

Yes. We repair and maintain laptops, desktop PCs, and all-in-one computers.

Can you install Windows and other operating systems?

Yes. We install and configure Windows, macOS, and Linux, including drivers and essential software.

Can I upgrade my laptop's RAM or SSD?

In most cases, yes. Our technicians will confirm compatibility before any upgrade and migrate your data safely.

How long does a computer repair take?

Many common repairs and diagnostics are completed the same day. More complex board-level work will be quoted with an estimated timeline.

Do you provide a warranty on repairs?

Yes. Every completed repair comes with a warranty on the parts and labor we provide. Ask our team for the exact terms for your service.
